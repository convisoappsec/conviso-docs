## Introduction

Release 4.22 delivered more flexibility for project-level security work and more precise risk intelligence for assets.

---

**_New Feature_**
## Custom Project Requirements

- Allows teams to tailor activities and descriptions within a project without changing the organization-wide template.
- Keeps governance templates consistent while making project backlogs relevant to each technical context.

---

**_Improvements_**
## Enhanced Data Classification

- Expanded Asset data-classification tiers from Public to Critical.
- Uses data sensitivity to improve risk prioritization for affected assets and vulnerabilities.

Learn more in the [Risk Score documentation](https://docs.convisoappsec.com/platform/risk-score).
