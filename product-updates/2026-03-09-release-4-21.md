## Introduction

Release 4.21 focused on visibility, usability, and governance across security workflows.

---

**_New Feature_**
## Security Gate in the Platform

- Made Security Gate configuration and monitoring available directly in the Platform.
- Supports global and asset-specific policies, severity thresholds, remediation time limits, and multiple vulnerability sources.
- Improves visibility into gate outcomes and the conditions that block a pipeline.

Learn more in the [Security Gate documentation](https://docs.convisoappsec.com/platform/security-gate).

---

**_Improvements_**
## Dark Mode

- Added Dark Mode across the Platform for a more comfortable, consistent experience during extended use.

---

**_Improvements_**
## Activity Evidence Navigation

- Simplified access to screenshots, documents, and other evidence attached to activity records.
- Reduces friction during validation, remediation, and compliance review workflows.
