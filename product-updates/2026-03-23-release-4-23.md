## Introduction

Release 4.23 made Secure by Design workflows more continuous and improved visibility into the delivery of findings to engineering backlogs.

---

**_New Feature_**
## Continuous Living Threat Modeling

- Introduced living, versioned threat-modeling artifacts driven by architecture diagrams.
- Keeps security requirements synchronized with architectural changes and preserves their evolution as an auditable history.

---

**_New Feature_**
## Defect Tracker Recent Deliveries

- Added visibility into recent synchronization events for defect trackers such as Jira, ClickUp, and ServiceNow.
- Helps teams troubleshoot delivery issues with detailed context and status information.
