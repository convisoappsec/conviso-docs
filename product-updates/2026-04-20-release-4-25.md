## Introduction

Release 4.25 improved the governance of automated testing, the consistency of DAST data, and the connection between security projects and engineering backlogs.

---

**_New Feature_**
## AI Pentest Policy

- Added policies for automated AI Pentest execution, including safety request limits and custom scheduling.
- Gives teams more control over how offensive testing is executed across their assets.

Learn more in the [AI Pentest documentation](https://docs.convisoappsec.com/ai-capabilities/ai-pentest).

---

**_Improvements_**
## Asset Filter Standardization

- Standardized Conviso DAST as a scanner source in filters and metrics.
- Improves consistency between scan coverage, dashboards, and asset filtering.

---

**_Improvements_**
## Projects and Jira Integration

- Improved the flow between Platform project requirements, activities, and Jira.
- Helps turn Secure by Design requirements into actionable engineering backlog items.
